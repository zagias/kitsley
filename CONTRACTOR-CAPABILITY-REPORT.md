## What “good” means

The comparison is the work expected of a contractor with 10 years of relevant experience, separately for each trade. Time served is context, not a numerical accuracy score. Kitsley has not demonstrated equivalent experience or qualifications.

**Current position:** useful, scoped technical reference assistance, plus a bounded illustrated bookcase workflow. General independent contractor-level judgement is not established.

The old “7 records / Partial” report measured inventory. This report measures the gap to doing a complete real job. There is no defensible percentage or number of training weeks to close it yet.

## How each complete case must be assessed

Each capability stays **not demonstrated** until a complete answer has been examined, including follow-ups. A keyword match cannot promote it.

1. Understand the request and reuse known facts; ask only missing questions that affect the answer.
2. Select the right material, product, hardware and method for the actual use and conditions.
3. Produce consistent, executable measurements, drawings, quantities and instructions where the engine supports them.
4. Diagnose competing causes and revise the plan when new facts arrive.
5. Identify dependencies and stop or hand off when site facts, evidence or a supported design operation are missing.

A critical dimensional, compatibility or safety error fails the case regardless of other scores. Passing a small fixed set does not establish general competence. Use unseen variations and independent qualified reviewers before advancing a trade to a higher level.

- Not assessed in this benchmark: live model answers on unseen cases and follow-up questions.
- Not assessed: independent qualified trade review or observed construction outcomes.
- The plywood intake regression is covered by automated tests: recognize the material/task, give relevant initial help and avoid repeating known facts. Browser verification is reported separately in the release notes.
- This work expands a reviewed reference engine and application logic. It is not model-weight training or autonomous expert certification.

## Example that exposed a gap

A knowledgeable assistant should recognize the material and task, explain the finishing choices, and ask about the intended appearance and existing surface/exposure only when missing. This is an interaction/decision-routing failure, even if the knowledge database already contains finishing facts.
